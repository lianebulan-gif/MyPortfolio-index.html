💻 Lesson 1: The Computing Industry

Introduction

  • Computing: Any activity involving computer machinery to achieve goals.
  • The Computing Industry: Covers technology research, development, sales, and production.

Core Areas

  • Computer Science: Theory of computation, algorithms, and design of computers.
  • Entertainment & Multimedia Computing: Focuses on digital content like games, VR/AR, and animation.
  • Information Technology: Focuses on development, maintenance, and data distribution systems.

Computing in the Philippines

  • Part of the Information Economy (IE), including ICT and Media sectors.
  • Trends: Massive growth in mobile transactions and ICT employment (~752,000 workers in 2021).

Career Opportunities

  • Roles: Software Developers, Web Developers, Security Analysts, and Database Administrators.
  • Salary Range: ~$61,000 to ~$140,000 annually (depending on specialization).
