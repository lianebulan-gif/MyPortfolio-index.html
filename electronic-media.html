📺 Lesson 6: Electronic Media

Introduction

  • Electronic media refers to media that uses electronic or digital technology to access content.
  • It plays a major role in communication, education, and entertainment.
  • Advancements in hardware and the internet have shifted how we consume information.

Traditional Media vs Electronic Media

  • Traditional Media: TV, radio, newspapers, billboards. (Declining reach).
  • Electronic Media: Internet, mobile apps, streaming platforms. (Interactive and on-demand).

Legal, Ethical, and Societal Issues

  • Copyright & Plagiarism: Protecting the rights of creators online.
  • Cyberbullying: Harassment using digital platforms.
  • Phishing & Piracy: Illegal distribution of media and data theft.

Philippine Cyber Law

  • R.A. 10175: Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012.
  • Addresses identity theft, cybersex, and online libel.
  • Aims to provide cybersecurity while protecting civil liberties.
