⚖️ Lesson 8: Ethics in the Computing Industry

Ethics in computing refers to moral principles guiding the responsible use and development of technology.

📜 Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)

  • Legal framework protecting creations of the mind (software, algorithms, digital content).
  • Encourages innovation by ensuring creators receive recognition and benefit.

© Copyrights vs ™️ Trademarks

  • Copyrights: Protects original works like code, websites, and multimedia.
  • Trademarks: Safeguards brand identity like logos and names.

🛡️ Patents

  • Protects new inventions and technical processes for a limited time.
  • Ethical concerns include restricting fair competition if misused.

💻 Digital Counterfeiting

  • Illegal duplication or distribution of software and digital products.
  • Causes financial loss and introduces security risks to systems.
